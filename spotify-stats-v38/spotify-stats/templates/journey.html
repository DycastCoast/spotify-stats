{{ j.range }} · how eras are found:

Each month gets a "mix": what share of it went on each artist. An era is a stretch of months whose mixes are alike. Every possible way of cutting the history into {{ j.k }} stretches (each at least 4 months) is tried, and the one where months fit their own stretch best wins. An era is named after the artist most typical of it: big in that era and much smaller outside it, so not every era gets named after your all-time favourite. "Broad" makes about one era per two years of history; "Detailed" nearly one a year.
