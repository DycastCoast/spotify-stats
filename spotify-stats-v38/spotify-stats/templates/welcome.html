Your stats start here

From now on the site keeps track of what you play on Spotify by itself. To see your whole history (years of it), it needs Spotify's full history file.

1

Get your full history

On spotify.com: Account → Privacy settings → tick Extended streaming history → request it. Spotify emails it within a few days (at most 30).

2

Upload it here

When the email comes, download my_spotify_data.zip and upload it on the Import page, without unzipping it.
