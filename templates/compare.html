How is this worked out?

Each person's listening is split into shares: if Kanye is 5% of {{ T }}'s time and 1% of yours, only the smaller 1% counts as shared. Add that up over every artist and you get the overlap. Two identical people would score 100%. It uses only {{ c.range }}, the time you both have history for, so whoever has more years of data doesn't look broader just for that. (Your history: {{ c.history_a[0] }} to {{ c.history_a[1] }}. {{ T }}'s: {{ c.history_b[0] }} to {{ c.history_b[1] }}.) By song rather than artist, the overlap is {{ '%.0f' % c.song_score }}%.
