How are trips found?

Spotify logs the country of the network you're on with every play. A trip is a run of days where another country makes up at least a quarter of your plays (a UK SIM abroad often still shows up as the UK, so it doesn't need to be most of them), lasting at least 2 days. A VPN can occasionally fake one. Only the full history export has countries, so the live tracking since then adds none.

Short trips where your phone mostly showed {{ t.home }} are caught from faint traces: plays from different countries a day or two apart (like a day each in two countries), a country on 2+ days close together, or a single day right next to a trip (counted as part of it, or as a stop on the way). Countries that keep popping up on their own on {{ 5 }}+ separate days are treated as VPN or network noise and ignored for this{% if t.noise %}: for you that's {% for code, name in t.noise %}{{ code | flag }} {{ name }}{{ ', ' if not loop.last }}{% endfor %}{% endif %}.
